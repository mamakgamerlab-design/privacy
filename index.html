# Privacy Policy **Developer:** MamakGamerLab **Effective date:** 11 October 2026 **Contact:** mamakgamerlab@gmail.com This Privacy Policy explains how MamakGamerLab ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you play our games. It applies to all games published by MamakGamerLab, including: - Vam-Busters (Android package: com.mbl.vambusters) By playing our games, you agree to the practices described in this policy. ## 1. Information we collect **Information you give us** - **Display name** – the name you enter when you create your account. It is shown to other players on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or other personal details. - **Country or region** – the country you choose when you create your account. It is shown as a flag next to your name on the leaderboard. **Information created when you play** - **Account ID** – a unique identifier we generate for your game account. - **Game progress** – your level, experience, coins, stamina, upgrades, unlocked and selected characters, and daily task progress. - **Scores and play statistics** – your high score and number of games played. - **Purchase records** – which in-game items you bought and the purchase confirmation needed to deliver them. **Information stored only on your device** - Game settings such as music and sound volume and your chosen language. - A local save file of your game progress. **Information we do not collect** We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID. Our games do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. ## 2. How we use information We use the information above to: - create and maintain your game account and save your progress; - show leaderboards, including your display name, country flag and score; - deliver in-game purchases and prevent fraud or cheating; - respond to support and account-deletion requests; - fix bugs and keep the game running properly. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. ## 3. How information is shared - **Other players** – your display name, country and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. - **Google Play** – in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never receive or store your card or bank information. Google's use of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). - **Unity** – our games are built with the Unity engine, which may collect limited technical and diagnostic information about your device. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). - **Service providers** – companies that host our game servers store data on our behalf and may only use it to provide that service. - **Legal reasons** – we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, our players, or the public. ## 4. Where your information is stored Depending on the game version, your account and progress are stored on your device, on our game servers, or both. Data stored only on your device is removed when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. ## 5. Data retention We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account ID, display name, country, game progress and scores from our servers within 30 days. We may keep purchase records for longer where required for legal, tax or accounting purposes. ## 6. Deleting your account and data You can delete your account and its data at any time in either of these ways: 1. **In the game:** open **Settings → Account → Delete Account** and confirm. 2. **By email:** send a request to **mamakgamerlab@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account", and include the name of the game and your Account ID (shown in the game's Settings screen). You do not need to have the game installed to make this request. Deleting your account is permanent. Your progress, coins and purchased items cannot be restored afterwards. ## 7. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email **mamakgamerlab@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days. ## 8. Security We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. ## 9. Children's privacy Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at **mamakgamerlab@gmail.com** and we will delete it. ## 10.

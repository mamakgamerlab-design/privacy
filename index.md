<h2>1. Information we collect</h2>
<p><strong>Information you give us</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Display name</strong> – the name you enter when you create your account. It is shown to
      other players on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or other personal details.</li>
  <li><strong>Country or region</strong> – the country you choose when you create your account. It is
      shown as a flag next to your name on the leaderboard.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Information created when you play</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Account ID</strong> – a unique identifier we generate for your game account.</li>
  <li><strong>Game progress</strong> – your level, experience, coins, stamina, upgrades, unlocked and
      selected characters, and daily task progress.</li>
  <li><strong>Scores and play statistics</strong> – your high score and number of games played.</li>
  <li><strong>Purchase records</strong> – which in-game items you bought and the purchase confirmation
      needed to deliver them.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Information stored only on your device</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Game settings such as music and sound volume and your chosen language.</li>
  <li>A local save file of your game progress.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Information we do not collect</strong></p>
<p>We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos,
or advertising ID. Our games do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use third-party
analytics or advertising SDKs.</p>

<p>We use the information above to:</p>
<ul>

  <li>show leaderboards, including your display name, country flag and score;</li>
  <li>deliver in-game purchases and prevent fraud or cheating;</li>
  <li>respond to support and account-deletion requests;</li>
  <li>fix bugs and keep the game running properly.</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.</p>

<p>Depending on the game version, your account and progress are stored on your device, on our game

<p>We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account,
